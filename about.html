About LCD Engineering Services

Hands-on experience, honest advice, and a name you can put behind the work.

The Short Version

Full-Time Crew Leader. Part-Time Side Business. Full Effort.

LCD Engineering Services is a mobile handyman and repair business — HVAC, boiler, electrical, plumbing, carpentry, general fixes — built around real, everyday field experience — not a side hustle learned from videos.

By day, I serve as an assistant chief leading a mobile engineering crew, responsible for diagnosing and resolving mechanical and HVAC issues in the field. That full-time role is where the judgment behind LCD Engineering Services comes from: knowing the difference between a five-minute fix and a job that needs real attention, and being straight with people about which one they're facing.

LCD Engineering Services exists to bring that same standard to homeowners and small businesses who need a small repair handled right — without waiting on a large company's schedule or paying for a service call they didn't need. This is repair work, not installation — I don't put in new equipment, no matter the trade, and no job is too small to take on.

Credentials

Real Licenses, Not Just Experience

HVAC and boiler work is backed by actual certifications — not just years of practice. Everything else is honest handyman-level repair, disclosed as exactly that.

EPA Section 608 Universal Certification

Federally certified to purchase and handle refrigerant across all appliance types — the credential required by law for any HVAC work involving refrigerant.

City of Chicago Stationary Engineer License

Licensed by the City of Chicago for boiler operation and maintenance work — not a handyman-level credential, an actual municipal license.

Electrical, plumbing, and carpentry work stays handyman-level — small fixes, not licensed trade installation. And across every trade, including HVAC and boiler: repairs only, never new equipment installations.
