About LCD Engineering Services

Property management & maintenance operations for multi-unit residential and mixed-use commercial portfolios across Chicago.

Who I Am

Real Experience. Operational Focus.

LCD Engineering Services is a property management and maintenance operations business focused on multi-unit residential and mixed-use commercial properties across the Chicago metropolitan area.

Hands-on HVAC and field operations experience is what keeps complex properties running smoothly — from routine maintenance and tenant coordination to major system diagnostics.

This isn't quick repairs for homeowners. It's operational support for landlords and property owners managing multiple units and complex mechanical systems.

Credentials

Real Licenses, Not Just Experience

HVAC and boiler work is backed by actual certifications — not just years of practice.

EPA Section 608 Universal Certification

Federally certified to purchase and handle refrigerant across all appliance types — the credential required by law for any HVAC work involving refrigerant.

City of Chicago Stationary Engineer License

Licensed by the City of Chicago for boiler operation and maintenance work — not a handyman-level credential, an actual municipal license.

HVAC and boiler systems are managed directly under these licenses. Electrical, plumbing, and other trade work is coordinated through vetted contractors as part of full portfolio maintenance.
