Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work full-time hours for this business?

LCD Engineering Services is a side business alongside a full-time role as an assistant chief for a mobile engineering crew. Because of that, appointments are primarily scheduled evenings and weekends — but reach out and I'll do my best to work around your timeline.

Are you licensed?

For HVAC refrigerant work, yes — EPA Section 608 Universal certified. For boiler work, yes — licensed as a City of Chicago Stationary Engineer. For electrical, plumbing, and carpentry, this is handyman-level repair work, not licensed trade work. Either way, if a job needs a licensed electrician or plumber for something beyond a minor fix, I'll tell you straight up and point you toward someone qualified.

Do you do installations?

No — repairs only, across every trade, no matter how small the job. If it's fixing what's already there, I'll take it on. If it's putting in new equipment or a new system, that's outside what I do, and I'll tell you upfront.

What kind of repairs do you handle?

Everything from a five-minute fix to a bigger repair job: thermostats, blower issues, boiler repairs, outlets and switches, running toilets and leaky faucets, doors and trim, drywall patches, and general troubleshooting. No job is too small.

Can you give me a second opinion on a quote I already received?

Yes — this is one of the most common requests. Send over what you were told and I'll take a look and give you a straight, no-obligation opinion.

How do I get a price?

Every job is different, so pricing is discussed after a quick description of the problem — by phone, email, or the contact form. No hidden fees, and nothing starts without your sign-off.
