Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work full-time hours for this business?

LCD Engineering Services is a side business alongside a full-time role as an assistant chief for a mobile engineering crew. Because of that, appointments are primarily scheduled evenings and weekends — but reach out and I'll do my best to work around your timeline.

Are you a licensed electrician or plumber?

No. LCD Engineering Services is handyman-level repair work — small fixes across HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and carpentry — not licensed trade installation. If a job turns out to need a licensed electrician, plumber, or full HVAC system install, I'll tell you straight up and point you toward someone qualified for it rather than take on work outside that scope.

What kind of repairs do you handle?

Small, handyman-level fixes: thermostats, blower issues, minor leaks, outlets and switches, running toilets and leaky faucets, doors and trim, drywall patches, and general troubleshooting. For major installs, full system replacements, or anything requiring a licensed trade contractor, I'll give you an honest referral.

Can you give me a second opinion on a quote I already received?

Yes — this is one of the most common requests. Send over what you were told and I'll take a look and give you a straight, no-obligation opinion.

How do I get a price?

Every job is different, so pricing is discussed after a quick description of the problem — by phone, email, or the contact form. No hidden fees, and nothing starts without your sign-off.

Ready to get it looked at?

Reach out with the details and I'll follow up with a straight answer and next steps.
